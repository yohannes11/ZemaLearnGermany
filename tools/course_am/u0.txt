## g0-vowels
Good news: German is written almost exactly as it is spoken. Once you know the sounds of the letters, you can read any word aloud, even one you have never seen before. ||| መልካም ዜና፦ ጀርመንኛ እንደሚነገረው ማለት ይቻላል በትክክል ይጻፋል። የፊደላቱን ድምፆች ካወቁ ማንኛውንም ቃል፣ ከዚህ በፊት ያላዩትንም ቢሆን፣ ጮክ ብለው ማንበብ ይችላሉ።
Each vowel (<em class="de">a, e, i, o, u</em>) has a <strong>long</strong> and a <strong>short</strong> sound. The spelling around the vowel tells you which one to use. ||| እያንዳንዱ አናባቢ (<em class="de">a, e, i, o, u</em>) <strong>ረጅም</strong> እና <strong>አጭር</strong> ድምፅ አለው። የትኛውን እንደሚጠቀሙ በአናባቢው ዙሪያ ያለው አጻጻፍ ይነግርዎታል።

## g0-umlauts
The two dots change the sound. <em class="de">ä</em>, <em class="de">ö</em> and <em class="de">ü</em> are vowels of their own, so <em class="de">schon</em> (already) and <em class="de">schön</em> (beautiful) are two different words. ||| ሁለቱ ነጥቦች ድምፁን ይቀይራሉ። <em class="de">ä</em>፣ <em class="de">ö</em> እና <em class="de">ü</em> የራሳቸው አናባቢዎች ናቸው፤ ስለዚህ <em class="de">schon</em> (አስቀድሞ) እና <em class="de">schön</em> (ቆንጆ) ሁለት የተለያዩ ቃላት ናቸው።
No umlaut on your keyboard? Write <strong>ae, oe, ue</strong>: <em class="de">Mädchen</em> → <em class="de">Maedchen</em>. You will also see this in email addresses and on forms. ||| ቁልፍ ሰሌዳዎ ላይ ኡምላውት የለም? <strong>ae, oe, ue</strong> ብለው ይጻፉ፦ <em class="de">Mädchen</em> → <em class="de">Maedchen</em>። ይህንን በኢሜይል አድራሻዎችና በቅጾች ላይም ያዩታል።
How to make them ||| እንዴት እንደሚነገሩ
The trick for ö and ü: say a sound you know, then round your lips without moving your tongue. ||| የö እና የü ብልሃት፦ የሚያውቁትን ድምፅ ይናገሩ፤ ከዚያም ምላስዎን ሳያንቀሳቅሱ ከንፈርዎን ያክብቡ።
