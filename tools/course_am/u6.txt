## g6-accusative
The <strong>direct object</strong> of a sentence (the person or thing the action is done to) is in the <strong>accusative</strong>. ||| የዓረፍተ ነገር <strong>ተሳቢ</strong> (ድርጊቱ የሚፈጸምበት ሰው ወይም ነገር) <strong>በአኩዛቲቭ (Akkusativ)</strong> ይገባል። በአማርኛ ተሳቢን «-ን» እንደምንጨምርበት (መጽሐፉን)፣ ጀርመንኛ መስተኣምሩን ይቀይራል።
Only <strong>masculine</strong> articles change: der → den, ein → einen, kein → keinen. Feminine, neuter and plural stay the same. ||| የሚቀየሩት <strong>የተባዕታይ</strong> መስተኣምሮች ብቻ ናቸው፦ der → den፣ ein → einen፣ kein → keinen። አንስታይ፣ ገለልተኛና ብዙ ቁጥር ባሉበት ይቆያሉ።

## g6-how-often
To say how well you do something, add a word after the verb: <em class="de">Ich koche gut.</em> With können, it goes before the verb at the end: <em class="de">Ich kann gut kochen.</em> ||| አንድን ነገር ምን ያህል በደንብ እንደሚሠሩ ለመናገር ከግሡ በኋላ ቃል ይጨምሩ፦ <em class="de">Ich koche gut.</em> (በደንብ አበስላለሁ)። ከkönnen ጋር መጨረሻ ላይ ካለው ግሥ በፊት ይገባል፦ <em class="de">Ich kann gut kochen.</em>
From worst to best: <em class="de">gar nicht → nicht so gut → ein bisschen → gut → sehr gut → super / toll</em>. ||| ከመጥፎ እስከ ምርጥ፦ <em class="de">gar nicht → nicht so gut → ein bisschen → gut → sehr gut → super / toll</em> (በፍጹም → ብዙም አይደለም → ትንሽ → ጥሩ → በጣም ጥሩ → ድንቅ)።
How often: <em class="de">nie → selten → manchmal → oft → immer</em>. These words usually come right after the verb: <em class="de">Ich koche oft.</em> ||| ምን ያህል ጊዜ፦ <em class="de">nie → selten → manchmal → oft → immer</em> (በፍጹም → አልፎ አልፎ → አንዳንድ ጊዜ → ብዙ ጊዜ → ሁልጊዜ)። እነዚህ ቃላት በአብዛኛው ከግሡ ቀጥሎ ይመጣሉ፦ <em class="de">Ich koche oft.</em>

## g6-vowel-e
Some common verbs change the vowel of their stem with <strong>du</strong> and <strong>er/sie/es</strong>. All the other forms stay regular. ||| አንዳንድ የተለመዱ ግሦች ከ<strong>du</strong> እና ከ<strong>er/sie/es</strong> ጋር የሥራቸውን አናባቢ ይቀይራሉ። ሌሎቹ ቅርጾች ሁሉ መደበኛ ናቸው።
