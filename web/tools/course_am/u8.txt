## g8-separable
Many travel verbs are separable: <em class="de">an|kommen, ab|fahren, ab|holen, ein|steigen, um|steigen</em>. In a normal sentence the small first part goes to the <strong>end</strong>: <em class="de">Ich komme um acht Uhr an.</em> ||| ብዙ የጉዞ ግሦች ተነጣይ ናቸው፦ <em class="de">an|kommen, ab|fahren, ab|holen, ein|steigen, um|steigen</em>። በተራ ዓረፍተ ነገር ውስጥ ትንሹ የመጀመሪያ ክፍል <strong>ወደ መጨረሻ</strong> ይሄዳል፦ <em class="de">Ich komme um acht Uhr an.</em> (ሁለት ሰዓት (8:00) ላይ እደርሳለሁ።)
The same in questions: <em class="de">Wann kommst du an? Holst du mich ab?</em> ||| በጥያቄዎችም እንዲሁ፦ <em class="de">Wann kommst du an? Holst du mich ab?</em>
With a modal verb (können, möchten …) the verb stays in one piece at the end: <em class="de">Kannst du mich abholen?</em> ||| ከረዳት ግሥ (können, möchten …) ጋር ግሡ መጨረሻ ላይ ሳይለያይ አንድ ሆኖ ይቆያል፦ <em class="de">Kannst du mich abholen?</em>

## g8-mit
To say how you travel, use <strong>mit</strong> + the vehicle. After mit, <em class="de">der</em> and <em class="de">das</em> become <strong>dem</strong>, and <em class="de">die</em> becomes <strong>der</strong>. (This is the dative case. For now, just learn these phrases.) ||| በምን እንደሚጓዙ ለመናገር <strong>mit</strong> + ተሽከርካሪውን ይጠቀሙ (በአማርኛ «በ…» እንደምንለው)። ከmit በኋላ <em class="de">der</em> እና <em class="de">das</em> <strong>dem</strong>፣ <em class="de">die</em> ደግሞ <strong>der</strong> ይሆናሉ። (ይህ ዳቲቭ (Dativ) ይባላል። ለአሁኑ እነዚህን አገላለጾች ብቻ ይማሩ።)
Walking is different: <em class="de">zu Fuß</em> (on foot). ||| በእግር መሄድ ይለያል፦ <em class="de">zu Fuß</em> (በእግር)።
