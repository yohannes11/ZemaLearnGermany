## g3-haben-essen
<em class="de">haben</em> (to have) loses its <strong>b</strong> in the du and er/sie/es forms. <em class="de">essen</em> (to eat) changes <strong>e → i</strong> in the same two forms. ||| <em class="de">haben</em> (ያለው መሆን) በdu እና በer/sie/es ቅርጾች <strong>b</strong>ን ያጣል። <em class="de">essen</em> (መብላት) በእነዚሁ ሁለት ቅርጾች <strong>e → i</strong> ይቀየራል።
haben in the present tense ||| haben በአሁን ጊዜ
essen ||| essen

du hast · er hat ||| du hast · er hat
haben loses the b ||| haben bን ያጣል

## g3-compounds
German loves joining nouns: <em class="de">der Apfel + der Kuchen = der Apfelkuchen</em> (apple cake). ||| ጀርመንኛ ስሞችን ማጣመር ይወዳል፦ <em class="de">der Apfel + der Kuchen = der Apfelkuchen</em> (የፖም ኬክ)።
The <strong>last</strong> noun is the main word. It gives the meaning and the article: a <em class="de">Kaffeetasse</em> is a cup (die Tasse), so it is <em class="de">die Kaffeetasse</em>. ||| <strong>የመጨረሻው</strong> ስም ዋናው ቃል ነው። ትርጉሙንና መስተኣምሩን የሚሰጠው እሱ ነው፦ <em class="de">Kaffeetasse</em> ሲኒ ነው (die Tasse)፤ ስለዚህ <em class="de">die Kaffeetasse</em> ይባላል። በአማርኛ «የቡና ሲኒ» እንደምንለው፣ ግን በተገላቢጦሽ ቅደም ተከተል።
Sometimes a small linking sound appears in the middle: <em class="de">Schokolade + Kuchen = Schokoladenkuchen</em>. ||| አንዳንድ ጊዜ መሃል ላይ ትንሽ አገናኝ ድምፅ ይገባል፦ <em class="de">Schokolade + Kuchen = Schokoladenkuchen</em>።
