## g9-perfect-haben
To talk about the past in spoken German you mostly use the perfect: <strong>haben</strong> in position 2 + the <strong>participle</strong> at the end: <em class="de">Ich habe Kaffee getrunken.</em> ||| በንግግር ጀርመንኛ ስለ ያለፈ ጊዜ ለመናገር በአብዛኛው ፔርፌክትን ይጠቀማሉ፦ <strong>haben</strong> በቦታ 2 + <strong>ፓርቲሲፕሉ</strong> መጨረሻ ላይ፦ <em class="de">Ich habe Kaffee getrunken.</em> (ቡና ጠጣሁ።)
Regular verbs: <strong>ge- … -t</strong>: machen → gemacht, kaufen → gekauft. Many irregular verbs: <strong>ge- … -en</strong>, often with a new vowel: trinken → getrunken, lesen → gelesen. ||| መደበኛ ግሦች፦ <strong>ge- … -t</strong>፦ machen → gemacht፣ kaufen → gekauft። ብዙ ደንብ የማይከተሉ ግሦች፦ <strong>ge- … -en</strong>፣ ብዙ ጊዜ በአዲስ አናባቢ፦ trinken → getrunken፣ lesen → gelesen።
Separable verbs put ge in the middle: einkaufen → ein<strong>ge</strong>kauft. Verbs ending in <strong>-ieren</strong> have no ge: telefonieren → telefoniert. ||| ተነጣይ ግሦች geን መሃል ላይ ያስገባሉ፦ einkaufen → ein<strong>ge</strong>kauft። በ<strong>-ieren</strong> የሚያልቁ ግሦች ge የላቸውም፦ telefonieren → telefoniert።

## g9-sein-haben
Most verbs make their perfect with haben. But verbs of <strong>movement from A to B</strong> use <strong>sein</strong>: gehen, fahren, fliegen, kommen, laufen, ankommen. ||| አብዛኞቹ ግሦች ፔርፌክታቸውን በhaben ይሠራሉ። ግን <strong>ከሀ ወደ ለ የሚደረግ እንቅስቃሴን</strong> የሚገልጹ ግሦች <strong>sein</strong> ይጠቀማሉ፦ gehen, fahren, fliegen, kommen, laufen, ankommen።
Also with sein: <em class="de">bleiben</em> (to stay), <em class="de">passieren</em> (to happen) and <em class="de">sein</em> itself: <em class="de">Ich bin in Berlin gewesen.</em> ||| ከsein ጋር የሚሄዱ ሌሎች፦ <em class="de">bleiben</em> (መቆየት)፣ <em class="de">passieren</em> (መከሰት) እና <em class="de">sein</em> ራሱ፦ <em class="de">Ich bin in Berlin gewesen.</em> (በርሊን ነበርኩ።)

## g9-war-hatte

## g9-time
<strong>von … bis</strong>: from … to: <em class="de">von 9 bis 17 Uhr</em>. <strong>ab</strong>: from … on: <em class="de">ab 8 Uhr</em>. ||| <strong>von … bis</strong>፦ ከ… እስከ፦ <em class="de">von 9 bis 17 Uhr</em> (ከሦስት እስከ አሥራ አንድ ሰዓት)። <strong>ab</strong>፦ ከ… ጀምሮ፦ <em class="de">ab 8 Uhr</em> (ከሁለት ሰዓት ጀምሮ)።
<strong>im</strong> with months and seasons: <em class="de">im März, im Winter</em>. <strong>seit</strong>: since, for: <em class="de">seit 2015</em>. ||| <strong>im</strong> ከወራትና ከወቅቶች ጋር፦ <em class="de">im März, im Winter</em>። <strong>seit</strong>፦ ከ… ጀምሮ / ለ… ያህል፦ <em class="de">seit 2015</em>።
